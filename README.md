## 🎓 About me

I'm a Computer Science student at the **University of Insubria** in Varese, nearing graduation with a thesis on **ARXivar and the world of Big Data**. I enjoy turning complex workflows into clear, reliable products that feel good to use.

I recently completed an internship at **DSC Group**, contributing to a complete demo environment for ARXivar Next: system provisioning, infrastructure configuration, AI feature modelling, and integration with Azure Document Intelligence.

## 🎯 What I'm focused on

- Building accessible, user-centred web and iOS experiences.
- Exploring AI, cloud technologies, workflow automation, and document management.
- Shipping complete portfolio projects: from product thinking and interface design to implementation and testing.
